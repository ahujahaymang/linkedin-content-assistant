📰 LinkedIn Newsletter Extractor

Extract articles from your LinkedIn newsletter page

📋 Instructions:
  1. Go to your newsletter page: https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/YOUR-NEWSLETTER-NAME/
  2. Make sure you can see the list of articles
  3. Open browser console (F12 or Cmd+Option+J)
  4. Click "Copy Newsletter Script" below
  5. Paste in console and press Enter
  6. The script will scroll and extract all articles
